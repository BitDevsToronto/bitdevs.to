BitDevs Toronto is an open meetup in Toronto for discussing Bitcoin research,
development, and the software people build on it.

Anyone is welcome. You do not need to be a developer, but the discussion is
technical and we do not stop to explain the basics.

### The format

The format is a Socratic seminar. There are no talks, no slides, and no project
pitches. One host walks the room through a list of recent developments, drawn
from the [Bitcoin Optech](https://bitcoinops.org/) newsletters published since
our last meeting, and everyone discusses them. You are expected to interrupt and
ask questions.

Meetings run about two hours, followed by an optional trip to a nearby bar for
anyone who wants it.

### Rules

Meetings run under the Chatham House rule. You are free to use what you hear,
but not to say who said it.

The discussion is never recorded, and we do not photograph attendees.

### Schedule

We meet roughly every two months on a weeknight evening. Each date is announced
here once the venue for it is confirmed.

### Agendas

The agenda for each meeting is published on this site in advance, and stays up
afterwards as a record of what was discussed. Suggestions for topics are
welcome. The site is [open source](https://github.com/BitDevsToronto/bitdevs.to),
so you can propose an addition directly.

### Other chapters

BitDevs started in New York City and now runs in more than forty cities. You can
find the rest at [bitdevs.org](https://bitdevs.org/cities).
